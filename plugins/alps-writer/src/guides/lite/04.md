<section_guide number="4" title="Demo Scenario" references="1,2">
<purpose>Work backward from the approved Desired Business Impact and Essential User Experiences to propose one executable Demo Scenario.</purpose>

<questions>
1. Ask only when the demo exposes a protected product decision that cannot be safely proposed: which boundary should apply?
</questions>

<workflow>
Read Sections 1 and 2. If Section 3 was written, read it too and keep every demonstrated claim inside that
explicit boundary.

Reverse-engineer the shortest scenario that makes every approved Essential User Experience observable.
Propose a concrete starting state, representative demo input, sequential user actions, and visible
expected results. Do not ask the user to supply those ordinary execution choices. Reuse shared setup
and actions when the experiences form one coherent flow. If connecting experiences would require an
unapproved product contract, keep separate execution blocks or ask one focused question when the
missing choice affects money, permissions, law or regulation, privacy or safety, irreversible data
meaning, an external promise, or the acceptance boundary.

For every step, show the user action, visible expected result, and the Essential User Experience it
demonstrates. Show how the complete scenario supports the Desired Business Impact without claiming
to prove that impact. The overall pass result requires every approved experience to be observable.
Present the complete generated scenario and its experience coverage, not only a summary, before
asking for approval.

Do not require a separate Learning Check, experiment plan, metrics framework, interview script, or
learning decision. Do not substitute a feature list, screen tour, subjective impression, or
implementation check. A passing demo proves only that the approved product behavior runs; do not
present it as proof of business impact or market validity. Do not omit an Essential User Experience
or silently fix a protected product decision absent from the approved Sections.
</workflow>
