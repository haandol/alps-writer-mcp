<section_guide number="6" title="Shared Product Principles">
<purpose>Set product-wide rules that several Features or screens must express consistently.</purpose>

<questions>
1. Who may view, change, confirm, share, or remove the work?
2. Which product-wide principles must every approved Feature or screen express?
</questions>

<workflow>
Derive candidate principles from the confirmed Primary Persona's approved use cases and Features.
Ask only about dimensions that apply: permissions and visibility, confirmation and reversibility,
failure and recovery, accessibility, privacy, and sensitive information. Failure and Recovery is
optional; ask about it only when the approved Features already imply a shared PoC behavior. Do not
invent policies or require a "Not applicable" placeholder for optional content.
</workflow>

<completion required="true">Confirm every applicable shared rule before saving. Optional failure and recovery content may remain empty.</completion>
</section_guide>
