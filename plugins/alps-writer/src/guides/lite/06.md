<section_guide number="6" title="Shared Product Principles">
<purpose>Set product-wide rules that several Features or screens must express consistently.</purpose>

<questions>
1. Who may view, change, confirm, share, or remove the work?
2. When something fails or is interrupted, what user work and confirmed result must remain?
</questions>

<workflow>
Derive candidate principles from the approved Features. Ask only about dimensions that apply:
permissions and visibility, confirmation and reversibility, failure and recovery, accessibility,
privacy, and sensitive information. Do not invent policies. Write "Not applicable" only after the
user confirms that a subsection does not apply.
</workflow>

<completion required="true">Confirm every applicable shared rule and every explicit non-applicable area before saving.</completion>
</section_guide>
